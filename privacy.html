GENEVA TAGALOG GROUP

Privacy & Data Use

Who is responsible?

This coordination service is operated for the Geneva Tagalog Group. Questions, correction requests or deletion requests can be made to an authorized administrator of the Geneva Tagalog Group.

Why we use information

Information is used only for internal coordination of public talks, visiting speakers, hospitality, volunteer assistance, assemblies, conventions and related group trips. It is not used for advertising, profiling or commercial sale.

Information we may collect

  • Speakers: name, congregation, location, contact details, talk availability and visit/travel information supplied for coordination.
  • Hospitality volunteers: first and last name, offers of assistance, availability and information needed for the offered service.
  • Trip participants: name, attendance, number of adults/children, transport, accommodation needs and optional comments.
  • Authorized coordinators: name, email address, role and application access status.

Please provide only information needed for the coordination purpose.

Who can see it?

Access is limited according to role. Authorized administrators and the relevant coordinators can access the information needed for their responsibilities. Volunteer, speaker and trip links do not provide access to the internal management workspace.

Storage and technical services

The application uses Supabase for authentication and database services and GitHub Pages to publish the web interface. These technical providers may process technical or stored information through infrastructure outside Switzerland depending on their service configuration and contractual arrangements.

Some public portals store a random response key or a participant/volunteer name locally on the user's device so the same browser can update or withdraw a response. This local information can be removed by clearing the browser's site data.

How long is information kept?

Information should be kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the coordination purpose, operational follow-up or necessary record keeping. Test information is removed when identified. Completed coordination data can be reviewed and deleted or reduced when it is no longer needed.

Your information

You may ask an authorized administrator to tell you what information is held about you, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion where appropriate. If you withdraw a volunteer or trip response through the portal, the application records that change so coordinators can update the arrangements.

Security and sharing

Internal access is restricted to authorized accounts and role-based permissions. Public portal links should be shared only with the intended participants. Information should not be copied or shared outside the coordination purpose unless this is necessary for the arrangement or the person concerned has been informed.
